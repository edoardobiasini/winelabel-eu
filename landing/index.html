What it does

Everything the regulation requires

WineLabel EU generates standalone, regulation-compliant digital label pages — accessible via QR code on your wine bottles.

Bare HTML Output

Clean, standalone pages with zero WordPress theme dependency. No cookies, no scripts, no tracking. Fully compliant.

Multi-Vintage

Each wine supports multiple vintages, each with its own ingredient data, nutritional values, and packaging details.

WooCommerce Optional

Works with WooCommerce products or as a standalone plugin with its own wine management. Your choice.
